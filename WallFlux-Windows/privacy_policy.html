Privacy Policy for WallFlux

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the WallFlux software application ("the App") for Windows devices, created by WeekendBuilder. This document explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

WallFlux is designed with user privacy as a primary focus. We strongly believe in minimizing data collection.

Personal Information

We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any personal information. The App does not require you to create an account with us, nor does it ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

Usage Data & Telemetry

We do not track your usage. The App does not use any internal analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services that send data back to the developers. All operations happen strictly on your local device.

2. How the App Functions (Device Permissions & Data Storage)

To function correctly, WallFlux requires certain permissions on your Windows device. These permissions are used exclusively to deliver the core functionality of the App:

  • Internet Access: The App requires an active internet connection solely to connect to the Wallhaven.cc API to search for, retrieve, and display wallpapers.
  • Local Storage (File System): When you choose to download or set a wallpaper, the App saves the image file to a designated folder on your local hard drive (typically your Pictures or AppData folder). The App also saves your configuration settings (such as filters, themes, and your personal Wallhaven API key, if provided) to your local storage.
  • System Personalization: The App interacts with the Windows operating system APIs to change your desktop wallpaper background when you explicitly request it or via the automated timer.

All data and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not sync this data to the cloud or have access to it.

3. Third-Party Services

WallFlux acts as a client for the Wallhaven service. When you use the App to search for or download wallpapers, the App makes direct HTTP network requests to Wallhaven's servers.

  • Wallhaven API: By using the App, your device communicates directly with Wallhaven. As a result, Wallhaven may collect standard web server data, such as your IP address, browser/client type, and request timestamps. If you choose to input your personal Wallhaven API key into the App, it is sent securely over HTTPS to authenticate your requests.
  • We encourage you to read the Wallhaven Privacy Policy to understand how they handle data processed through their API.

4. Data Security

Because WallFlux does not collect or transmit your personal data to us, the security of your data relies entirely on the security of your local Windows device. We recommend keeping your operating system updated and using appropriate security software. All network traffic between the App and the Wallhaven API is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information (which the App does not collect), please contact us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in the App's features, third-party services, or legal requirements. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our GitHub repository or the support channels listed on the Microsoft Store page.
