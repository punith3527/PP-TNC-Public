Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. The short version

Nimbus has no servers. Your photographs are encrypted on your phone and stored in your own Telegram Saved Messages, under your own Telegram account. We never receive your photographs, we never receive your encryption key, and there is no account to create with us.

2. Information We Collect

None. Nimbus collects no personal information, sends us no data, and has nowhere to send it to. Specifically, the app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting.

The app reads the following on your device, and it stays on your device:

  • Photographs and videos in your device's media library
  • The date, size, name and album each one was filed under
  • The location recorded in a photograph's own EXIF data, read only at the moment that photograph is backed up, so it can be sealed into the encrypted record
  • How full your device's storage is

3. Where your photographs go

When you back a photograph up, Nimbus encrypts it on your phone and sends the encrypted result to your own Telegram Saved Messages — a conversation with yourself, on your account.

  • The encryption happens before anything leaves the device.
  • Telegram stores the encrypted file. Telegram cannot read it.
  • We cannot read it either. We have no copy and no key.
  • Your use of Telegram is governed by Telegram's own Privacy Policy.

4. Your recovery key

Nimbus gives you a 27-character recovery key when your vault is created. It is the only thing that can open your vault on another phone.

  • It is generated on your device.
  • It is stored only on your device, in Android's hardware-backed key store.
  • It is never transmitted anywhere, to us or to anyone else.
  • If you lose it and lose this device, your photographs cannot be recovered — not by you, not by us, not by Telegram. That is the cost of nobody else being able to read them.

5. Permissions

Nimbus asks for the following Android permissions, and uses them only for these purposes:

  • Photos and videos — to show your library and to back it up
  • Media location — to read where a photograph was taken, so that fact is kept inside the encrypted record rather than lost
  • Internet and network state — to reach Telegram, and to honour your "Wi-Fi only" setting
  • Notifications — to show backup progress
  • Foreground service (data sync) — so an upload continues with the screen off instead of being stopped halfway
  • Ignore battery optimisations — optional, and asked for only if you turn on backing up while the app is closed. Android pauses background work for optimised apps, which stops a backup the moment the screen goes off.

6. Third-Party Services

Telegram — the app talks to Telegram's API to sign you in and to store your encrypted files in your own Saved Messages. This is your account and your relationship with Telegram.

Google Maps — the Places screen draws a map. Map tiles are requested from Google when you open that screen. Your photographs are never sent to Google; only the map area being displayed is requested.

There are no other third-party services. No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting.

7. Data Deletion

You are in control of everything:

  • Delete individual items from inside the app
  • Sign out, which erases this device's copy of your keys
  • Delete the messages directly in Telegram, from any device
  • Uninstall the app, which removes everything it stored locally

8. Children's Privacy

Nimbus does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The app is intended for general audiences, and requires a Telegram account, which has its own age requirements.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
